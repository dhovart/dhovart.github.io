    A study app for exam revision. Free software under the AGPL.

    I write a devlog alongside it: why a CRDT sits at the centre of a study app, how a design system got smaller and more accessible by leaving things out, where AI sped the work up and where it quietly made it harder.

    Try it in the browser Desktop and mobile apps Devlog Source
  • cargo-local-registry since 2021, Rust

    A Cargo subcommand that builds a local registry from a lockfile, for building Rust projects on machines without internet access. Started by Alex Crichton in 2016, handed to me in 2021. I keep it working through Cargo's internal API changes, added an add command for single crates, and moved CI and prebuilt binaries to GitHub Actions. Over 300,000 downloads on crates.io.
